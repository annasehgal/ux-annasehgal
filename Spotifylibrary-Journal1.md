# Journal 1: Spotify's Library
I often listen to songs across different music streaming platforms, mainly using the desktop version of Spotify on my MacBook while connected to my headphones. A few days ago, I was browsing for a song from an older playlist that I had saved a few years ago, but I could not remember the playlist's name. I decided to scroll through my playlists in my Spotify library without noticing the playlist filter at the top. While scrolling, I noticed multiple playlists appearing in my library, including playlists from artists I followed. It also included Spotify Blends and playlists that were no longer available.

As I scrolled through my library on my laptop while working on an assignment, I noticed that even with the search feature, I was unable to find the particular playlist I was looking for. I also did not notice the playlist filter at the top because there were many things on the dashboard competing for my attention, including the play button and recently played playlists. The panel on the left was also difficult to navigate because some playlist names were longer and harder to read. I found it inefficient that some playlists were still appearing even though they were no longer available. The playlist I was looking for either seemed to have been deleted or simply did not show up, even after scrolling up and down a few times. This made the library harder to navigate than I expected, especially since I have been using Spotify for years.
